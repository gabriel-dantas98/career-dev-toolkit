# Build Promotion Packet

## Goal

Build an evidence-bounded promotion packet from validated local CareerOS records without scoring readiness or inventing impact.

## Non-goals

- Recommend promotion, infer level, or produce a readiness verdict.
- Fabricate enough cards to meet a target count.
- Include unsupported impact, unresolved dates, or missing evidence as established fact.
- Persist, sync, deploy, or publish the packet.

## Inputs

- A local record selection or review period accepted by the CLI.
- Linked evidence, delivery types, period data, and explicit evidence gaps.

## Outputs

- Ten to twelve evidence-backed work cards when enough records qualify.
- Separate collapsed community, recognition, and unresolved-work summaries.
- An explicit `insufficient_evidence` status when fewer than ten work cards qualify.

## Voice/Tone

Evidence-proportional and plain. Present missing evidence as a limitation, not a negative verdict.
