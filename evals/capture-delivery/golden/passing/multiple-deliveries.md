### Reduced CI duration

- Situation: The synthetic CI pipeline took 18 minutes.
- Task: Shorten feedback time.
- Action: I profiled the pipeline and parallelized independent suites.
- Result: Median duration fell from 18 minutes to 11 minutes, about 39%.
- Evidence: 18 minutes and 11 minutes.
- Confidence: complete

### Reduced onboarding ramp-up

- Situation: Onboarding relied on tribal knowledge.
- Task: Create a maintained learning path.
- Action: I documented and organized the path used by three teams.
- Result: Median ramp-up fell from 8 days to 5 days, or 37.5%.
- Evidence: 8 days, 5 days and three teams.
- Confidence: complete
